EqDaily is an offline daily check-in where you decide the questions. Answer them once a day, then see how your mood, habits and symptoms move over time.

Everything stays on your device. There is no account, no sync, no network access and no Android permissions at all. Export a backup any time and your data stays yours.

* Your own questions: slider, number, yes/no, text, checkboxes, single choice and time
* One quick card per question, saved as you answer; step back a day to fill in a gap
* Skipped is not "no": a question you leave alone is never counted in any statistic
* Insights over 7, 30, 90 days or all time, each showing "n of m days answered"
* Streaks, a weekly summary and a calendar heatmap
* Patterns: gentle hints such as how your mood differs on days you were short on sleep
* A first-launch guide with suggested starter questions
* Backup and restore as a plain zip of CSV files, so your data is never locked in
* Light and dark themes, following the system
