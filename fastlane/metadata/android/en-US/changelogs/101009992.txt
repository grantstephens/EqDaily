Typing is nicer: the keyboard no longer covers the field, text boxes grow as you write, dragging the list closes the keyboard, and the last text question has a Done button. Time questions use the Android clock picker and can have a default start time. Insights adds Patterns, streaks, a weekly summary and a calendar heatmap. New optional Settings → Privacy switch (off by default) shares anonymous screen-view counts; this adds the INTERNET permission.
